# Credits and third-party notes

- NaNDL model and formulas: NaN GD, https://nandl.pages.dev/ (the mod re-implements the public formulas).
- Default constants k_T, k_U, k_C and the `.fwc` format: Frame Window Counter by hyper-5 (MIT), https://github.com/hyper-5/frame-window-counter
- Replay-analysis approach (move one input, re-simulate, validate): inspired by the open-source Auto Frame Window Analyzer (MIT) and similar tools.
- Replay formats: libGDR (maxnut/GDReplayFormat) and GDR-converter (maxnut/GDR-converter), fetched by CMake.
